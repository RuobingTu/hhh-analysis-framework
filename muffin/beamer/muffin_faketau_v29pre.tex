% ---------------------------------------------------------------- method
\begin{frame}{What MUFFIN is}
  \framesubtitle{MUltivariate Fake-Factor INference (Andreou, Colling, Winterbottom; CMS-PAS-TAU-25-001)}
  \small
  The binned fake factor is replaced by a continuous, multi-dimensional
  per-object weight:
  \[
    w_{\rm MUFFIN}(z)=\frac{p^{\rm data}_{\rm pass}(z)-p^{\rm sim}_{\rm pass}(z)}
                           {p^{\rm data}_{\rm fail}(z)-p^{\rm sim}_{\rm fail}(z)}
  \]
  \begin{itemize}
    \item This is \emph{the same quantity} our map delivers as $FR/(1-FR)$: the
      denominator is inclusive-loose, so $FR/(1-FR)=N_{\rm pass}/N_{\rm fail}$.
      Only the estimation changes -- continuous in 7 variables instead of
      $p_T\times|\eta|$ cells per prong.
    \item Estimated with a single BDT classifier trained on pass vs.\ fail with
      \textbf{signed} weights ($+1$ data, $-w_{\rm ana}$ genuine-$\tau$ simulation).
      The stationary point of that weighted log-loss is $s=A/(A{+}B)$ over the
      \emph{net} densities, so $w_{\rm MUFFIN}=s/(1-s)=e^{\rm margin}$ exactly.
    \item Inputs are the poster's list verbatim: $\tau_h$ decay mode,
      $p_T^{\rm jet}/p_T^{\tau}$, $p_T^{\tau}$, $N_{\rm jets}$, $N_{b}$,
      $\eta$, $\phi$. No mother-jet flavour axes (dropped from our FR method).
    \item Applied per event exactly like \texttt{tauFR\_weight\_2d}: a
      dependency-free C++ header, validated against the python model to $3\times10^{-6}$.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Uncertainty model}
  \small
  Five components, following the poster; 30 trainings in total
  (1 nominal $+$ 20 bootstrap $+$ 7 hyper-parameter $+$ 2 background-subtraction):
  \vspace{0.3em}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{lll}
    \toprule
    component & how & value (1$\tau_h$0l VR) \\
    \midrule
    statistical & 20 Poisson-bootstrap retrainings & 2.4\% \\
    modelling   & 7 hyper-parameter variations & 1.2\% \\
    bkg.\ subtraction & $\pm10\%$ on the subtracted genuine-$\tau$ MC & 1.8\% \\
    non-closure $+$ extrapolation & VR closure residual & 2.5\% \\
    \midrule
    \textbf{total} & quadrature & \textbf{4.0\%} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
  \vspace{0.4em}
  Per bin of $p_T^{\tau}$, $H_T$ and $p_T^{\rm jet 1}$ the MUFFIN band is a flat
  \textbf{11.4\%}, while the binned map grows from 13.7\% to \textbf{16.5\%}
  into the tails, where its cells run out of events.
\end{frame}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- ttbar transfer
\begin{frame}{Validation I: does the fake factor travel between channels?}
  \framesubtitle{$t\bar t$ MC truth, fake $\tau_h$ only -- no data-driven subtraction needed}
  \small
  Measure the fake factor on $t\bar t$ MC in 1$\tau_h$1l, predict 1$\tau_h$0l.
  724k / 270k fake $\tau_h$, so the transfer is tested well below a percent.
  \vspace{0.3em}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{lcccccc}
    \toprule
    & integral & $p_T^{\tau}$ & $p_T^{\rm jet}$ & $|\eta|$ & $N_{\rm jets}$ & $p_T^{\rm miss}$ \\
    \midrule
    \multicolumn{7}{l}{\textit{transfer} 1$\tau_h$1l $\to$ 1$\tau_h$0l} \\
    MUFFIN            & \textbf{1.018} & \textbf{1.8\%} & 3.3\% & \textbf{2.6\%} & \textbf{1.9\%} & \textbf{3.5\%} \\
    binned map        & 1.031 & 14.8\% & 3.0\% & 3.4\% & 4.1\% & 5.0\% \\
    inclusive-only    & 1.046 & 8.8\%  & 16.9\% & 4.9\% & 4.6\% & 6.4\% \\
    \midrule
    \multicolumn{7}{l}{\textit{reference} 1$\tau_h$0l $\to$ 1$\tau_h$0l, out-of-fold} \\
    MUFFIN            & 1.000 & 0.5\% & 4.1\% & 5.2\% & 0.6\% & 2.4\% \\
    binned map        & 1.000 & 16.2\% & 0.5\% & 1.7\% & 3.2\% & 2.6\% \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
  \vspace{0.3em}
  The $t\bar t$ fake factor transfers well: the inclusive value differs by 4.5\%
  between channels, and a differential fake factor absorbs most of it --
  \textbf{1.8\% residual for MUFFIN}, 3.1\% for the binned map, 4.6\% for a constant.
  MUFFIN transfers \emph{better}, because the channels differ largely in
  $N_{\rm jets}$/$p_T$ and it is differential in those.
\end{frame}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------- SPANet score
\begin{frame}{Validation II: overall closure is the same, the score region is better}
  \framesubtitle{1$\tau_h$0l inclusive -- black points MUFFIN, red line the binned $F_F$}
  \begin{columns}[T]
    \begin{column}{0.52\textwidth}
      \begin{center}\figH{1.0}{1tau0l_spanet.png}\end{center}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
      \small
      \begin{itemize}
        \item Same events, same MC-prompt stack, same anti-ID selection and
          subtraction -- only the fake factor differs, so the gap between the
          two curves \emph{is} the difference between the methods.
        \item Integrated yields agree to \textbf{0.02\%} (1$\tau_h$0l VR) and
          \textbf{0.08\%} (inclusive): MUFFIN does not change the normalisation.
        \item It changes the \textbf{shape}. In the high-score region the binned
          $F_F$ drifts up to $\sim$1.4, while MUFFIN stays within the band.
        \item Over every SPANet ProbHHH4b2tau training the closure improves by
          \textbf{1.4--2.7\%}; $p_T^{\tau}$ improves from 11.9\% to \textbf{3.8\%}.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What the fake-lepton-only configuration shows}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Turning the data-driven fake-$\tau$ template off and keeping only the
      fake-lepton estimate, the prediction still describes the data across
      $N_{\rm jets}$, $N_{\rm fatjets}$, $H_T$, $p_T^{\tau}$, $p_T^{\rm jet1}$
      and $p_T^{\ell}$.
    \item So in this channel the \textbf{fake-lepton contribution is small}, and
      the \textbf{pure-MC description is already good} -- the data-driven
      fake-$\tau$ template is a refinement on top of a sound baseline, not a
      patch covering a mismodelling.
    \item This is also why the fake-lepton systematic does not drive the
      1$\tau_h$1l uncertainty.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Summary}
  \small
  \begin{itemize}
    \item MUFFIN reproduces the binned $F_F$ in normalisation
      (\textbf{0.02--0.08\%} on the total yield) and improves the \textbf{shape},
      most visibly in $p_T^{\tau}$ (11.9\% $\to$ 3.8\%) and in every SPANet
      ProbHHH4b2tau training (\textbf{1.4--2.7\%}).
    \item Its systematic is \textbf{smaller}: a flat 11.4\% per bin against
      13.7--16.5\% for the map, which degrades into the tails.
    \item The cross-channel transfer is validated on $t\bar t$ MC:
      \textbf{1.8\%} residual for MUFFIN, 3.1\% for the binned map.
    \item 2$\tau_h$0l: the (tight,tight) region holds 111 events. The same fake
      factor closes in the 526-event single-tight sideband at
      \textbf{0.998 $\pm$ 0.056}, so the method is validated in that channel too.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
